“Shared credit changes the allocation. Broader journeys can also bring previously unattributed conversions into reporting. These are separate changes.” A single-winner method could also gain coverage from additional valid journey information. This is not a capability exclusive to multi-touch.

Show actual G0 N and its proportion of all conversions in the final evidence notes when available. The illustrative levels are Q2 0/12/22/36%, Q3 0/14/24/38%, all using the original G0 denominator. Values show cumulative changes, not independent effects.

Current filters corresponds to L2. Longer history relaxes incumbent lookback windows but retains the model's annual pre-conversion cutoff (L3). Broader eligibility corresponds to L4; broader sources to L5. L0 customer/reference union is a separate linkage comparison. Hold the model fixed when isolating input changes and disclose refitting separately. Real results need not be monotonic.

More allocation is not new sales or improved accuracy. Positive-only normalization can give a full credit to weak positive scores. Check score strength, No Response sensitivity, timing and linkage validity; No Response does not prove observed exposure.
